## Session Priority & Preference Grades

When multiple sessions can match an event, use the following priority tiers to decide what to submit.
Higher-priority sessions should always be submitted first; lower-priority ones fill remaining slots.

### 🔴 Priority 1 — Top Priority (Talks, Workshops & Demos)
These are your flagship topics. Always submit them when the event theme allows.

| Session Base | Format | Focus |
|-------------|--------|-------|
| Your Flagship Session Title | Talk / Demo | Short description of your highest-priority talk and the outcomes attendees get |
| Your Hands-On Workshop | Workshop / Lab | Practical, guided session that helps attendees apply your approach |
| Your Signature Deep Dive | Talk / Demo | Core expertise area with a strong technical angle and optional live demos |

### 🟠 Priority 2 — High Priority
Strong secondary sessions. Submit when Priority 1 topics are not the best fit or when multiple submissions are allowed.

| Session Base | Format | Focus |
|-------------|--------|-------|
| Your Secondary Session | Talk | Supporting topic that complements your main expertise |
| Your Industry Case Study | Talk / Workshop | Lessons learned, patterns, and real-world implementation details |
| Your Trend / Strategy Session | Talk | Broader perspective tied to your domain experience |

### 🟡 Priority 3 — Standard Priority
Solid sessions for broader or complementary themes.

| Session Base | Format | Focus |
|-------------|--------|-------|
| Your Tools Session | Talk | Productivity, workflow, or tooling improvements |
| Your Architecture Session | Talk | Design patterns, scalability, or reliability themes |
| Your Practice-Focused Session | Talk / Demo | Tactics, techniques, and applied guidance |

### ⚪ Priority 4 — Situational
Submit only when an event theme specifically calls for it.

| Session Base | Format | Focus |
|-------------|--------|-------|
| Your Niche Topic | Talk | Specialized subject for targeted audiences |
| Your Intro / Vision Session | Talk | Broader, less technical, or inspirational topic |

## Audience Level Guidance

- **Technical audiences (L200-L400)**: Always include **live demo** mentions in session abstracts and descriptions. Live demos significantly improve acceptance rates. Emphasise hands-on, code-level content.
- **Introductory audiences (L100)**: Keep content accessible and vision-oriented. Demos may be included but should not be the focus — emphasise concepts, business value, and real-world impact instead.

## Topic-to-Session Mapping

When composing a submission, map the event's themes to your ready-to-adapt sessions:

| Event Theme | Suggested Session Base | Priority | Adaptation Notes |
|-------------|----------------------|----------|------------------|
| Your main specialty | Your Flagship Session Title | P1 | Explain why this topic is a strong match for your target events |
| Emerging trend in your field | Your Trend / Strategy Session | P2 | Tailor examples to the audience's maturity and interests |
| Hands-on workshop request | Your Hands-On Workshop | P1 | Adjust for workshop length, prerequisites, and expected outcomes |
| Architecture / engineering theme | Your Architecture Session | P3 | Emphasize technical depth, patterns, and implementation tradeoffs |
| Productivity / tooling theme | Your Tools Session | P3 | Highlight practical workflows and measurable improvements |
| Niche or specialized track | Your Niche Topic | P4 | Submit only when there is a clear topic fit |
